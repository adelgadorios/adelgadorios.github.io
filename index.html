Selected research

Studies and systems

Projects spanning foundational user research, spatial computing, and multimodal AI.

Children's AR Interaction Models

CHI 2026

Designed and conducted an open-ended elicitation study with 20 children, then coded 663 interaction proposals to identify intuitive behaviors and inform future AR interaction design.

  • Qualitative coding and affinity mapping
  • Whole-body and external-object interaction findings
  • First-author peer-reviewed publication

Adult AR Interaction Preferences

N = 30

Led a study of 30 adults across 17 virtual-object manipulations, analyzing 1,017 proposals to uncover natural interaction preferences and modality-switching behavior.

  • Affinity diagramming and statistical analysis
  • 1,017 analyzed interaction proposals
  • Novel techniques beyond current headset capabilities

AR Virtual Agent Study Platform

Unity · Magic Leap 2

Built an AR research platform for studying children's perceptions of virtual agents during collaborative tasks, including eye-tracking behavior and synchronized headset–tablet data collection.

  • Unity/C# and ARFoundation development
  • Eye-tracking-driven agent behavior
  • HTTP-based multi-device synchronization

Multimodal AI for Clinical Assessment

AI · Computer Vision

Developing a multimodal system that combines speech, gesture, and video to support analysis of communicative intent in people with aphasia.

  • MediaPipe gesture extraction
  • Vision-language model integration
  • Audio, video, and text synthesis
