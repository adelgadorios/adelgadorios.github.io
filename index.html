Research Projects

Elicitation Study of Children's AR Interaction Models

Sept 2024 – Present

  • Led an open-ended elicitation study with 20 children (ages 9-12) to investigate their interaction preferences with virtual objects in AR headsets.
  • Constructed a conceptual model of children's expectations, revealing preferences for whole-body interaction and the use of external objects, in contrast to adult models.
  • Analyzed 663 interaction proposals using qualitative coding and affinity diagramming, uncovering that children create narratives to contextualize their interactions.

Elicitation Study of Adult AR Interaction Preferences

Feb 2024 – Dec 2024

  • Designed and conducted an elicitation study with 30 adults to discover natural interaction preferences for 17 common virtual object manipulations in AR.
  • Quantified and analyzed 1,017 interactions, finding that while hand gestures were the primary choice (82%), 52% of secondary choices involved a modality switch (e.g., to voice).
  • Identified novel interaction paradigms not supported by current commercial headsets, including thinking, air-blowing, and air-drawing.

Applied AI for Clinical Assessment

Feb 2025 – Present

  • Developing a multimodal AI system to analyze speech and gestures, aiming to infer communicative intent in individuals with aphasia.
  • Engineering a computer vision pipeline with MediaPipe to detect and classify co-speech gestures from the AphasiaBank video dataset.
  • Integrating a vision-language model (Qwen2.5-VL) to perform multimodal inference and generate annotated transcripts.

AR Virtual Agent Study Platform

Oct 2024 – Present

  • Built a Unity AR application to investigate children's perceptions of virtual agents during collaborative tasks.
