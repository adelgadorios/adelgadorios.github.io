About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of South Florida, specializing in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). My research focuses on understanding and improving how people interact with emerging technologies like Augmented Reality headsets. I am passionate about conducting foundational user research to build more intuitive and effective mixed-reality experiences.
